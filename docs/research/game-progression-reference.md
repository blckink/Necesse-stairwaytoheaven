## Early Game: Surface & Cave Tier

### Ore & Equipment Progression

| Tier | Ore | Bar | Tools | Armor | Notes |
|------|-----|-----|-------|-------|-------|
| Surface | Copper | Copper Bar | Copper Pickaxe | Copper Armor | First crafting tier (4 ore → 1 bar at Forge) |
| Cave I | Iron | Iron Bar | Iron Pickaxe | Iron Armor | Second progression stage |
| Cave II | Gold | Gold Bar | Gold Pickaxe | Gold Armor | Final easy-cave tier |
| Cave II | Frost Shard | — | Frost Shard Pickaxe | Frost Shard Armor | Snow biome special material; bridges to deep caves |

**Defense Scaling:** Early armor (Copper) provides 5–7 defense; armor reduces damage by 0.5 per point.

### Surface Biomes (5 total)
- **Forest** (Oak) — Villages, caves, deep caves
- **Plains** (Birch) — Villages, caves, deep caves
- **Snow** (Spruce) — Villages, caves, deep caves; Frost Shard ore source
- **Swamp** (Willow) — Villages, caves, deep caves
- **Desert** (Palm) — Villages, caves, deep caves

**Cave Access:** Place **Cave Ladder** on terrain in any biome; many villages contain direct cave entrances.

## Mid Game: Deep Cave Tier (Tungsten Era)

### Unlocking Deep Caves
1. Defeat **Pirate Captain** (7th surface-tier boss) → drops **Deep Cave Ladder**
2. Or craft at **Tungsten Workstation** (requires Tungsten Bar)

### Deep Cave Ores & Equipment

| Ore | Source | Pickaxe Requirement | Armor | Special Notes |
|-----|--------|---------------------|-------|----------------|
| Tungsten | Any Deep Cave | Quartz Pickaxe or better | Tungsten Armor | Mid-tier deep-cave material (4 ore → 1 bar) |
| Glacial | Snow Deep Caves | Tungsten Pickaxe | Glacial Armor | Cold biome variant |
| Mycelium | Swamp Deep Caves | Tungsten Pickaxe | Mycelium Armor | Biome-specific |
| Ancient Fossil | Desert Deep Caves | Tungsten Pickaxe | — | Biome-specific ore |

**Deep Cave Boss Progression:**
1. **Reaper** — Drops Gateway Tablet, Shadow Essence; unlocks Incursions
2. **Cryo Queen** — Drops Gateway Tablet, Cryo Essence
3. **The Cursed Crone** — Drops nature/summoner weapons
4. **Pest Warden** — Drops Gateway Tablet, Bio Essence
5. **Sage and Grit** — Drops Gateway Tablet, Primordial Essence; opens Temple
6. **Fallen Wizard** — Drops Gateway Tablet, Altar Dust; unlocks Incursions

**Deep Cave Threats:** Wall-piercing mobs (Deep Cave Spirit in Forest, Cryo Flake in Snow, Sandworm in Desert), Cavelings (ore/mineral drops).

**Armor:** ~40–60 defense for Tungsten/Glacial/Mycelium sets (increased set bonuses).

### Crafting Infrastructure
- **Tungsten Workstation** — Required to craft Deep Cave Ladder, Tungsten Landscaping Station
- Decorative/specialty blocks for building

## Late Game: Incursion Tier

### Unlocking Incursions
1. Defeat **Fallen Wizard** → drops **Gateway Tablet**
2. Craft **Fallen Altar** (requires Tungsten Bars, etc.)
3. Place tablet in altar → opens portal to incursion level

### Incursion Mechanics
**Three objective types:**
- **Hunt Incursions:** Kill 100 enemies
- **Extraction Incursions:** Mine 100 ore blocks (enemies spawn progressively)
- **Trial Incursions:** Direct boss fights (Sun Arena, Moon Arena)

**Endgame Boss Encounters:**
- Mother Slime, Night Swarm, Spider Empress, Moonlight Dancer, Sunlight Champion, Crystal Dragon
- **Ascended Wizard** — Final boss (rolls credits)

**Incursion Ores & Armor:**
- **Nightsteel Ore** (Graveyard) — Nightsteel Armor (~90 defense)
- **Spiderite Ore** (Spider Castle) — Spiderite Armor (~90 defense)
- **Slimeum** — Slime Armor (~82–90 defense)

**Loot:** Orange-tier legendary weapons, upgraded armor sets, higher-tier tablets, essences, crafting materials.

## Settlements & Quests (Brief)

**Settlements** are player-built villages (flag placement). Recruit **Settlers** who perform jobs: farming, animal husbandry, forestry, crafting. Requires housing (beds + rooms + doors). **Elder NPC** provides sequential boss-defeat quests (unique rewards). Settler happiness affects prices and productivity; unhappy settlers (< 50%) strike. Settlements can be expanded at higher tiers with coins + special resources (Demonic Bars, Frost Shards, gems).

## Gemstones & Special Materials

**Common:** Amethyst, Emerald, Ruby, Sapphire, Topaz  
**Rare:** Obsidian, Quartz, Life Quartz  
**Exotic:** Omni-Crystal (advanced crafting)  
**Essences:** Shadow, Cryo, Bio, Primordial (incursion gating)  
**Legendary:** Demonic Bars (Evil's Protector), Void Shards (Void Wizard)

## Key Progression Gates

1. **Cave Ladder** → Access caves
2. **Pirate Captain defeat** → Deep Cave Ladder (crafting or drop)
3. **Tungsten/Frost Shard tier** → Survive deep caves
4. **Fallen Wizard defeat** → Gateway Tablet → Incursions
5. **Incursion completion** → Endgame boss sequence
